01 — About Eneya

Built on the Copperbelt, moving for the region.

Eneya Logistics Ltd is a Zambian haulage company based in Ndola's Industrial Area, at the centre of the Copperbelt's mining and chemical supply chain. We run a mixed fleet of flatbeds, tankers and side-tippers built for one job: getting heavy, hazardous and time-sensitive cargo where it needs to be, intact and on schedule.

Every truck that leaves our yard is backed by a workshop team that strips, rebuilds and pressure-tests our own engines and running gear — so the fleet you book is one we trust with our own name on the door.

"We don't just haul freight. We carry the next stage of production for every client on our route. That's why every move counts."

— Director, Eneya Logistics Ltd
